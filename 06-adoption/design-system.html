PalengkeHub Design System

One token layer for the Expo app, the admin dashboard and the public site. Every colour on this page is taken from the basket logo. Nothing here is a framework default.

73components
38colour tokens
3surfaces served
2type families
Light theme is the product. The dark theme in this file is defined but not yet used in the app. It exists so nobody has to invent a dark palette later, and so the old navy dark mode (#0F0F1E) can be retired in one commit.

Elevation

This system separates surfaces by contrast, not by shadow. A card is warm white on woven paper, so it needs no drop shadow at all. Shadows are for things that genuinely float.
5 levels
e0 none
e1 hairline
e2 float
e3 overlay
bar
The primary button uses a solid 3px offset in brand-dark instead of a blur. It sinks on press. That is the signature interaction of the system.
One convention shadowColor is always #261006 with a fractional opacity, never rgba() plus shadowOpacity: 1. Every level ships a matching Android elevation.

Icons

One family. The app currently mixes Ionicons, MaterialIcons and Feather on the same screen. These are drawn on a 24px grid with a 2px round stroke, matching the logo outline weight.
click to copy
Sizes xs 15 inline with text, sm 18 in chips and rows, 24 default, lg 30 in empty states. Stroke stays 2px except xs which uses 2.4 so it survives.

Form Controls

Inputs

Filled, not outlined-on-white: a woven tan fill with a 2px border. On focus the field turns to card white and gains a 4px brand ring, so the active field is obvious on a busy screen.

Input Add-ons

Prefix for the peso sign, suffix for the unit, attached button for actions that belong to the field.
₱/ kg

Search

The pill search field is the first thing on the home screen. The clear button only appears once there is text.

Password

Show and hide toggle inside the field. Used on vendor login and on the admin dashboard.
Never a strength meter with a rainbow gradient. A hint line is enough.

Number Stepper

Quantity control on the product screen and in the cart. Fires hapticSelection() on every tick.
1
Minimum 1. Deleting is a separate destructive action, not a step below one.
Target 40px buttons inside a 48px pill.

OTP and PIN

Six cells, auto advance, backspace steps back. Used for GCash confirmation and for the vendor pickup PIN.
Display face Baloo 2 at 24px so the digits read as a code, not as body text.

Selection

Checkbox, radio and switch. The check mark is ink on brand, never white on brand, because the orange is light.
Checkbox 26px box, radius 8.
Radio and switch switch track 56 by 32, thumb travels 24.

Verdict Chips

The single most PalengkeHub thing in the system. A verdict is computed from the roster of stalls selling that product, never hand-set, and a product with no roster gets no chip at all.
Mura Katamtaman Mahal Pinakamura
Mura
5 percent or more below the market average for that product, unit-normalized. Leaf soft on leaf dark.
Katamtaman
Within plus or minus 5 percent of the average. Wicker soft on ink-2, deliberately quiet.
Mahal
5 percent or more above the average. Tomato soft on tomato dark. This is the only red judgement in the app.
Contract one function computes the verdict, and the card, the detail screen and the compare sheet all call it. No screen may claim a verdict its own roster does not support.
