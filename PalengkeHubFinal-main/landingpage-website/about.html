About PalengkeHub

Bringing the Lipa City Public Market online — one stall at a time.

Ang Misyon

Making the palengke accessible to everyone

PalengkeHub was built with a simple goal: to bring the authentic experience of the Lipa City Public Market into the digital world. We want to make it easier for everyone — from busy professionals to students — to order fresh produce, home goods, and pasalubong from trusted local vendors without the hassle of walking through the crowded market.

Our platform connects verified sellers from the Lipa City Public Market directly to buyers through a simple order-and-pickup system. No delivery fees, no complicated shipping — just order online, pay securely via GCash, and pick up your tinda at the market.

Ang Aming Kwento

From a university project to a real solution

01

The Idea

We started PalengkeHub as a student project — a reaction to the everyday struggle of finding fresh, affordable goods in Lipa City without spending hours inside the market.

02

The Build

Four teammates with different strengths came together: design, development, database management, and quality assurance. We built the app from scratch with one goal in mind — simplicity.

03

The Market

We partnered with local vendors at the Lipa City Public Market to make sure the products are real, the sellers are verified, and the pickup experience is as smooth as possible.

04

The Future

Today, PalengkeHub continues to grow — expanding to more stalls, more goods, and more happy customers who love the convenience of ordering their palengke online.

Ang Aming Mga Halaga

What we stand for

Trust & Verification

Every seller on PalengkeHub is verified. We make sure that when you order, you're buying from a real stall owner at the Lipa City Public Market.

Sariwa at Quality

We prioritize freshness. Because you pick up your order directly from the vendor, you get the same quality as if you picked it yourself.

Suporta sa Lokal

By ordering through PalengkeHub, you're supporting local vendors and small businesses of Lipa City — keeping the palengke alive and thriving.

Simple at Mabilis

Walang kumplikado. Order, bayad, sundo — tatlong hakbang lang. We keep the process as simple as possible for both buyers and sellers.
